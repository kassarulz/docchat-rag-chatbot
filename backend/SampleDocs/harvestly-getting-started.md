# Harvestly – Getting Started Guide

Harvestly is a fictional farm management app for polytunnel and greenhouse growers. This guide covers setting up your farm, inviting your team and recording your first crop.

## Creating your farm

After signing up, Harvestly asks for your farm name, country and currency. The currency is used for all cost and sales reports and cannot be changed later without contacting support, so choose it carefully. Most growers in Sri Lanka choose LKR.

Next, add your growing areas. Harvestly calls each enclosed growing space a **tunnel**. For every tunnel you enter a name (for example "Tunnel A"), the floor area in square metres and the irrigation type: drip, sprinkler or manual. You can add open-field plots as tunnels too; set the irrigation type to manual.

## User roles

Harvestly has four roles:

- **Admin** – full access, including billing, user management and deleting data.
- **Manager** – can create and edit crops, stock, mixes and harvests, and see all reports. Managers cannot change billing or remove users.
- **Worker** – can add daily logs, fertilizer mixes and harvest records, but cannot edit or delete anything older than 24 hours.
- **Viewer** – read-only access to dashboards and reports. Useful for owners or investors.

To invite someone, go to **Settings → Team → Invite user**, enter their email and choose a role. The invite link expires after 7 days. An admin can deactivate a user at any time; deactivated users keep their history but can no longer sign in.

## Recording your first crop

Go to **Crops → New crop** and choose the tunnel, the crop type (for example tomato, bell pepper or cucumber), the variety, the planting date and the number of plants.

Harvestly then creates a crop timeline. Each day, workers add a **daily log** with notes, a photo (optional) and any issues such as pests, leaf curl or irrigation faults. Issues marked as "urgent" send a notification to all managers.

A crop stays active until you mark it as **finished**. Finished crops are kept for reporting but no longer appear on the daily dashboard.

## The dashboard

The dashboard shows today's tasks, active crops per tunnel, low-stock warnings for fertilizer, and the harvest total for the current week. Managers and admins also see the cost per kilogram for each active crop, which combines fertilizer, labour and other recorded expenses.

## Data export

Admins can export any report to CSV from the report's menu (the three dots in the top-right corner). Exports include all records in the selected date range. There is no limit on the number of exports.
