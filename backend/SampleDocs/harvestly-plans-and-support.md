# Harvestly – Plans, Billing and Support FAQ

## What plans are available?

Harvestly has three plans, billed monthly in the farm's currency:

| Plan | Tunnels | Users | Price (LKR / month) |
|------|---------|-------|---------------------|
| Starter | Up to 3 | Up to 3 | Free |
| Grower | Up to 15 | Up to 10 | 4,500 |
| Estate | Unlimited | Unlimited | 12,000 |

All plans include fertilizer tracking, daily logs and harvest records. The **Grower** and **Estate** plans add cost-per-kilogram reports, CSV export and the audit log. Only the **Estate** plan includes the finance module (budgets, labour costs and sales margins) and priority support.

## Is there a free trial?

Yes. New farms get a 30-day free trial of the Grower plan. No payment details are needed to start the trial. At the end of the trial, the farm moves to the Starter plan automatically unless an admin chooses a paid plan. Data is never deleted when a trial ends, but tunnels beyond the Starter limit become read-only.

## How do I pay?

Admins can pay by card or by bank transfer. Bank transfers must include the farm ID as the payment reference and can take up to 2 working days to be confirmed. Card payments renew automatically each month.

## Can I get a refund?

Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time and stay active until the end of the paid month. Harvestly does not give partial refunds for unused days, except when a payment was taken in error, in which case support refunds it in full within 7 working days.

## How do I change or cancel my plan?

Go to **Settings → Billing**. Upgrades take effect immediately and you pay the difference for the rest of the month. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing month.

## Is my data safe?

Farm data is stored in an encrypted database with daily backups kept for 30 days. Each farm's data is isolated from other farms. Harvestly never sells or shares farm data with third parties.

## Does Harvestly work offline?

The mobile web app caches today's tasks and lets workers add daily logs and harvest records without a connection. Records sync automatically when the phone reconnects. Fertilizer mixes require a connection, because stock levels must be checked before a mix is saved.

## How do I contact support?

Starter and Grower plans get email support with a reply within 2 working days. Estate plans get priority support by email and phone with a reply within 4 working hours. Support hours are Monday to Friday, 8:30 AM to 5:30 PM Sri Lanka time.
